Your Privacy

Privacy & Cookie Policy

How Bavarian Auto Werkz handles your information when you book, call, or browse. Serving Fresno, CA.

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Overview

Bavarian Auto Werkz ("we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the cookies our website uses. We do not sell your personal information.

Information We Collect

We only collect information you choose to share with us:

  • Contact details you submit through our service-request form (name, email, phone, vehicle details, and your message).
  • Appointment details you enter in our online booking tool.
  • Information you provide when you call or email us.

We use this information solely to respond to your request, schedule and perform service, and communicate with you about your vehicle.

Cookies & Similar Technologies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We use a limited set:

  • Essential cookies / local storage: remember your cookie-consent choice so we don't ask again. These are always active because the site cannot function properly without them.
  • Third-party functional cookies: our online booking calendar and contact form are provided by trusted third parties and may set cookies to make scheduling and submissions work. These load so you can book an appointment or reach us.
  • Fonts: we load web fonts from Google Fonts to render the site; this may expose your IP address to Google per their policies.

We do not use advertising cookies or sell data to advertisers.

Managing Your Choices

When you first visit, a banner lets you Accept or Decline non-essential cookies. You can change your mind anytime by clearing this site's data in your browser settings, which will prompt the banner again. Most browsers also let you block or delete cookies directly.

Third-Party Services

Your use of our booking and form tools is also subject to those providers' privacy policies. We share your information only as needed to provide the service you request and never for unrelated purposes.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information you have submitted to us. To make a request, contact us using the details below.
